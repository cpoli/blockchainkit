Examples#

This gallery walks through blockchainkit.wallets, one experiment per breakthrough on the wallets history page: the error-detecting codes behind addresses, from BCH codes and Verhoeff’s check digit to Base58Check, Bech32 and Bech32m; PBKDF2, scrypt and the brain wallets that had neither; hierarchical deterministic keys, mnemonic seed phrases and derivation paths; and shared control of keys, from P2SH multisig and threshold ECDSA to social recovery and ERC-4337.

Each script is self-contained and runs with python examples/wallets/<section>/<script>.py.
