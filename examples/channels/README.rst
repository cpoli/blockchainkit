Examples
========

This gallery walks through ``blockchainkit.channels``, one experiment per
breakthrough on the :doc:`channels history page
</history/channels_breakthroughs>`: Reed-Solomon codes and data-availability
sampling; payment channels from Spilman's to Lightning, Sprites and eltoo,
with watchtowers; balance probing in a channel network; atomic swaps, BTC
Relay and the bridge exploits; and Plasma, sharding, and zk and optimistic
rollups.

Each script is self-contained and runs with
``python examples/channels/<section>/<script>.py``.
