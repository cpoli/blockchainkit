Examples
========

This gallery walks through ``blockchainkit.privacy``, one experiment per
breakthrough on the :doc:`privacy history page </history/privacy_breakthroughs>`:
Chaum's mix-nets and Tor's onion routing; ring signatures, key images and
stealth addresses; k-anonymity, address clustering and Monero's
traceability; CoinJoin, TumbleBit and Tornado Cash; and the hidden amounts
of Zerocoin, confidential transactions, RingCT and Mimblewimble.

Each script is self-contained and runs with
``python examples/privacy/<section>/<script>.py``.

.. raw:: html

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="David Chaum wanted to hide who writes to whom, even from someone watching every wire. A sender wraps its message in one layer of encryption per mix, innermost for the last. Each mix collects a batch, removes one layer from every message, and sends them on in a random order. The bytes change at every hop and so does the order, so an observer cannot match what goes in to what comes out.">
