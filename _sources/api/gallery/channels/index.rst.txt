Examples
========

This gallery walks through ``blockchainkit.channels``, one experiment per
breakthrough on the :doc:`channels history page
</history/channels_breakthroughs>`: Reed-Solomon codes and data-availability
sampling; payment channels from Spilman's to Lightning, Sprites and eltoo,
with watchtowers; balance probing in a channel network; atomic swaps, BTC
Relay and the bridge exploits; and Plasma, sharding, and zk and optimistic
rollups.

Each script is self-contained and runs with
``python examples/channels/<section>/<script>.py``.

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Paying for a stream by the second on chain costs a transaction, and a fee, per payment. Jeremy Spilman&#x27;s channel needs two. The payer locks the channel&#x27;s capacity in a 2-of-2 output, after the payee has signed a refund that returns everything to the payer at a lock time. Each payment is a new transaction from that output, paying the payee a little more; the payer signs it and hands it over, and the payee countersigns and publishes only the last one. Mike Hearn built the scheme into bitcoinj the same year.">
