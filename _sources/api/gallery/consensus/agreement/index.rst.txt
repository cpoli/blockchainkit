In each round they exchange values, propose any value held by a strict majority, and adopt a proposal they see, or flip a coin if they see none. A decision follows once enough processes propose the same value.">

.. raw:: html

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Real networks are usually fast but sometimes not. Dwork, Lynch and Stockmeyer modelled this as partial synchrony: message delays are unbounded until an unknown Global Stabilization Time (GST), and bounded by an unknown Delta afterwards. A leader-based protocol keeps safety at all times and waits out each leader with a timeout. Doubling the timeout every failed view guarantees it eventually exceeds Delta, whatever Delta is.">

With n = 3f + 1 replicas, every quorum of 2f + 1 overlaps every other in at least f + 1 replicas, so in at least one honest replica. A replica prepares a value after 2f matching prepares and commits after 2f + 1 matching commits; no two honest replicas can commit different values in one view.">
